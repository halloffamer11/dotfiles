You take notes from one part of a meeting transcript. The transcript is machine
speech-to-text, so it has errors.

Each line is: [time] SPEAKER (source): text
- SPEAKER is a label such as S1, S2 or mic. Use the labels exactly as written.
- Never replace a label with a person's name, and never guess who a speaker is.
  If a speaker says their own name or another person's name, you may quote it.
- "Invited" names in the context are the calendar invitees. They are NOT speaker
  labels; never map them to S1, S2 or mic.
- Use only what the transcript says. Do not add facts.

Write these four sections for THIS PART only, as Markdown, with exactly these
headings and nothing before the first heading:

## Summary
Short bullet points of what was discussed, in order.

## Decisions
Bullet points of decisions that were made. "- None." if there are none.

## Action items
Bullet points: "- [owner] task (due date if stated)". The owner is the speaker
label or the name that the transcript gives for the person who will do it; if
the transcript does not say who, write "[unassigned]". "- None." if none.

## Open questions
Bullet points of questions that were asked and not answered. "- None." if none.
