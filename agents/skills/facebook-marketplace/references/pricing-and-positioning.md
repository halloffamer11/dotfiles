# Pricing and positioning

## Purpose and inputs

Turn seller facts, media findings, product evidence, competitor observations,
and seller priorities into a defensible price proposal and a clear way to
present the item. Use the available competitive asking range,
replacement-price context, item condition, completeness, proof or warranty,
availability, local demand signals, seller priorities, and evidence
limitations. Keep each input's provenance in the listing record.

Marketplace displays are usually asking prices. Do not claim that an observed
asking price proves demand, value, or a completed sale. Do not claim completed
sales without reliable, item-matched completed-sale evidence. A thin or mixed
sample supports a broad, qualified recommendation, not false precision.

## Pricing output

Produce the following, at the precision the evidence supports:

- **observed range:** The comparable asking-price range, the sample's relevant
  differences, and whether any completed-sale evidence was available.
- **recommended asking price or range:** A proposal that accounts for confirmed
  condition, included items, item-specific proof, warranty, availability, and
  replacement-price context.
- **Rationale and confidence:** The few facts that move the recommendation,
  plus the limits on confidence such as sparse results, uncertain variants, or
  a broad search area.
- **Negotiation considerations:** Include a public asking position, a possible
  adjustment path, or a need for a seller floor only when the seller asks for
  negotiation help. Keep private floors in `listing.md`, never in buyer copy.
- **Alternatives:** When seller priorities differ, show the resulting proposal,
  such as a faster-sale option and a wait-for-the-right-buyer option. Do not
  fabricate a difference the market evidence cannot support.

Treat every price and positioning recommendation as a proposal until the user
approves it. All outputs are proposals until that approval. Record the
recommendation and its evidence in `listing.md`; only the user can approve a
price for use in an external listing.

## Positioning choices

Choose a position that a buyer can verify. Prefer, in order of relevance:

- A specific product strength supported by product evidence and applicable to
  the confirmed item.
- Clear evidence of this item's condition or a stated test result.
- A precise list of included items, including material omissions.
- A truthful convenience the seller can provide, such as an actual delivery
  or shipping option, expressed through platform settings rather than copy.
- A competitive gap that the seller can close with better identification,
  photos, condition proof, testing evidence, or accessory clarity.

Do not turn a model capability into proof that this unit works, or turn a
competitor's vague listing into a reason to overstate the seller's item. The
position should answer the buyer's material uncertainty with evidence the
seller owns.

## Compact proposal for the seller

Default to a compact conversational proposal, not a report or packet. Include:

1. Recommended price.
2. Proposed title.
3. Proposed description.
4. Photo guidance that addresses the most material remaining uncertainty.
5. Only the next decision or question that matters.

Add rationale only when it materially explains the recommendation or the user
asks for it. Keep detailed observations, limitations, alternatives, source
links, and provenance in `listing.md` so the seller does not need to review a
report to act.

## Effort calibration

If project configuration supplies an improvement threshold, recommend a bundle
of additional seller work only when competitive-market evidence suggests the
likely improvement exceeds that threshold. The evidence might show that nearby
comparable listings command meaningfully more when they document a material
accessory, test result, or condition detail that the seller can also verify.

Do not estimate seller time. Do not create task-level ROI. If the evidence does
not suggest that the threshold is exceeded, proceed with the truthful evidence
already available or name the optional improvement without recommending it.

Read any threshold from project configuration. This portable procedure does
not embed a location, currency amount, or seller preference.
