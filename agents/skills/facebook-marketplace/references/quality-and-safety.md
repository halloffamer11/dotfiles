# Quality and safety procedure

## Truthfulness

Check every buyer-facing field and platform selection against its evidence and
the canonical listing record before and after entry.

| Topic | Required check |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Brand, model, variant, and category are seller-confirmed, observed from the item, or supported by cited item-matched evidence. Mark inference or unknowns instead of presenting them as fact. |
| Condition | State only seller-confirmed or directly observed condition, wear, and defects. A photo does not establish cleanliness, internal condition, or functionality. |
| Testing | Say an item was tested, working, paired, charged, or otherwise functioning only with seller confirmation or direct recorded evidence for this item. |
| Accessories | List only items seller-confirmed or directly observed as included; plainly disclose material omissions and limitations. |
| Price and availability | Use only `pricing.approved`; state availability, pickup, delivery, and shipping terms only as the seller confirmed them. Do not imply a private negotiation floor. |
| Warranty | Claim a warranty only when seller confirmation or applicable cited terms support both its existence and whether it transfers. |
| Defects | Disclose known material defects, damage, incomplete parts, and limitations in clear language; do not crop, word, or categorize them away. |

If a claim lacks support, remove it, mark it unknown for the seller, or ask the
one consequential question that would resolve it. Do not transform inference,
product capability, or a platform suggestion into a fact about this unit.

## Privacy

Review the entered text, selected media, visible browser state, screenshots,
and any saved posting notes before sharing or publishing. Exclude or redact:

- Receipts, order identifiers, transaction references, and warranty claim IDs.
- Serial numbers unless their disclosure is necessary and the seller has
  explicitly approved it; prefer safe model evidence instead.
- Street addresses, precise pickup location, personal phone numbers, personal
  email addresses, account names, payment details, and private contacts.
- Image metadata when it could disclose location, device, or other unnecessary
  personal information.
- Reflections, backgrounds, labels, screens, and documents that reveal a
  person, home address, workplace, contact information, or account data.

Keep location and contact disclosure to the minimum required by the live form
and approved design. Do not put private evidence, negotiation notes, or browser
screens containing account information in buyer-facing copy or routine reports.

## Approval-scope and stale-approval checks

Read the scope, decision, actor, and time of the canonical
`approvals.listing_design`, `approvals.populated_form`, and
`approvals.publication` entries. An approval covers only the visible state and
external action it names. Material changes to price, included items, condition,
title, description, photo set, fulfillment, or required Facebook fields need
renewed review for the affected action.

Treat an approval as stale when any covered value changed, a platform-generated
change materially alters the visible listing, the live form surfaces an
unresolved consequential choice, or the populated state cannot be verified
after a browser interruption. Preserve work, identify the difference, and
obtain the appropriate renewed approval. Never rationalize a new decision from
an older, broader, or delegated approval.

## Platform safeguards

Use the live form as the authority for its available fields and restrictions,
but do not bypass warnings, security controls, login or identity checks,
category restrictions, confirmation screens, or any platform safeguard. Stop
on an unexpected restriction or warning, record its exact visible text when
safe, and ask the seller to complete any required manual platform action or
choose a revised approved design.

## Distinct external actions

Browser form entry, populated-form review, and publication are distinct
external actions with distinct approvals. Current listing-design approval
permits only reversible entry of resolved choices. A populated-form approval
permits readiness review only. Publication requires its own current explicit
decision immediately before the publish action; unavailable seller status or
general delegated judgment is not an approval.

## Recoverable stop report

When stopping or handing off, report only what is useful and safe:

1. **Completed work:** fields entered, media uploaded, and review status.
2. **Preserved artifacts:** record location, approved media paths/order, safe
   screenshots or field mapping, and unchanged draft state.
3. **Exact blocker:** the observed login, security, validation, warning,
   restriction, UI, upload, or browser issue--without guessing its cause.
4. **Safest next action:** the reversible retry, renewed review, or seller
   decision needed; never a workaround around a safeguard.
5. **Manual step required:** the precise action only the seller must perform,
   such as completing sign-in or a platform security check.

Do not claim publication, discard the approved media plan, or expose private
data in a recovery report. On return, re-inspect the live state and re-run the
approval-scope checks before proceeding.

## Baseline-derived rationalization

The controls below are limited to failures actually observed in Task 2's
baseline; they do not assert additional baseline findings.

| Observed baseline failure | Control justified by that evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Responses treated a delegated instruction as sufficient while omitting design review, populated-form review, or post-review publication approval. | Require ordered, distinct approval gates and reject delegated judgment as publication authority. |
| Sparse-input responses often asked first or described future work instead of providing a provisional read and a draft-or-deepen path. | Keep seller-facing preflight and recovery output concise, concrete, and action-oriented. |
| A still-photo response claimed cleanliness, testing, and working condition. | Require provenance for condition and test claims; photos alone do not prove those claims. |
| A category-guidance response recommended extra work without an observable threshold. | Request additional evidence only when it resolves an identified consequential buyer uncertainty. |
| A category-guidance response misstated visible specifications and made unsupported category claims. | Recheck buyer-facing values against observed or cited evidence and preserve unsupported claims as unknown. |
