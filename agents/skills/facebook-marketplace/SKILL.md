# Facebook Marketplace

## Operating principle

Build the strongest truthful listing supported by the available evidence. Keep
the canonical listing record current, make useful progress from partial inputs,
and leave consequential choices to the seller.

## Default SOP

1. Read the project configuration (seller area, thresholds, fulfillment and
   payment policy) and existing listing record. Use
   [listing-record.md](references/listing-record.md).
2. Interpret the available facts and media, then give a useful provisional read.
   Use [intake.md](references/intake.md) and [media.md](references/media.md).
3. Research the product, product line, and category buyer decision factors. Use
   [product-research.md](references/product-research.md).
4. Assess geographically relevant Facebook competition and missed opportunities.
   Use [marketplace-assessment.md](references/marketplace-assessment.md).
5. Recommend whether the listing is ready or which next improvement is likely to
   matter. Ask one seller question when needed.
6. Develop pricing, positioning, and a compact listing proposal. Use
   [pricing-and-positioning.md](references/pricing-and-positioning.md).
7. Draft buyer-facing copy and apply the required human-writing pass. Use
   [listing-copy.md](references/listing-copy.md).
8. Present the compact proposal and obtain conversational listing-design approval.
9. Populate the live Facebook form. Use
   [browser-posting.md](references/browser-posting.md).
10. Present the populated form, obtain its approval, then request separate
    publication approval.
11. Publish only after explicit authorization and record the outcome.

Honor the user's current direction and update the record with whatever the work
establishes. When information is sparse, begin with a useful starter and a clear
path to either draft now or improve the listing with one input, then ask at most
one focused consequential seller question. After the reply, choose the next
useful action. Unknown information stays unknown; a provisional recommendation
states its assumptions. Keep detailed research in the record unless it materially
explains the recommendation or the seller asks for it.

## Approval boundaries

- Do not treat a recommendation or delegated judgment as approval of a price,
  listing design, populated form, or publication.
- Do not populate Facebook until the user approves the current listing design.
- After entry, the seller must review and explicitly approve the populated form's
  visible state.
- Do not treat populated-form approval as publication approval.
- Do not publish without a separate explicit authorization after that review.
- Reconfirm affected choices after a material change to approved content.

Use [quality-and-safety.md](references/quality-and-safety.md) before any external
action.

## Optional independent review

When uncertainty or judgment risk justifies the overhead, obtain an independent
product, pricing, skeptical-buyer, or marketing review through any available
consultation or delegation capability. Verify its claims against the record and
sources. Consultation never replaces seller approval.
