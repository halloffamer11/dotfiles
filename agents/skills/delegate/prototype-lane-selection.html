PROTOTYPE: which lane does a job go to?

Every lane draws on one subscription meter. A meter has a remaining share of the week and a cycle left share of the week. Pace = remaining ÷ cycle left: 1.0 means spending evenly, above 1 means quota will expire unused, below 1 means burning too fast. The question this page answers: given your preference order for a class, how much should the meters be allowed to change the pick, and who sets the threshold? Four policies, same meters, same class. Press buttons, watch the pick change.
